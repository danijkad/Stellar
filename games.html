Where you'll find us

Our games

World of Warcraft is home, but the community plays a lot more than that.

Our home

World of Warcraft

Where Stellar started. We raid as a guild every week and run keys whenever people are online.

  • Current-tier progression raiding with a focus on steady, clean improvement.
  • Mythic+ groups for every key level, from first-timers to high pushes.
  • Log reviews and class help for anyone who wants to improve.
  • Alt runs, old content farms, and achievement nights.

Hero shooter

Marvel Rivals

A growing part of the community. Stacks form most evenings.

  • Six-stacks for ranked play.
  • Quick play for warming up and trying new heroes.
  • Comp planning and team-up practice.

Everything else

Other games

If enough members are playing something, it gets a channel.

  • Community game nights with co-op and party games.
  • Launch-day sessions for big releases.
  • Suggest a game in the Discord to get people together.
