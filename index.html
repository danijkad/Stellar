Who we are

Shining brightly, together

Just as stars light up the night sky, Stellar aims to stand out in the World of Warcraft community through how we treat people. We raid, we run keys, and we play plenty of other games together, all with the same values.

Whether you're chasing cutting edge or just want a friendly group for the evening, there's a spot for you here.

Our values

Inclusiveness

Every skill level, every background. If you're here to play and be decent to people, you belong here.

Growth

We help each other get better, with honest feedback and patient teaching, whether that's your first raid or your hundredth key.

Fairness

Clear rules, open loot decisions, and the same standards for officers and new members alike.
