Our mission

About Stellar

Why we chose the name, and what we hold ourselves to.

Mission statement

The name “Stellar” embodies the essence of our guild's vision and values. It signifies excellence, brilliance, and a commitment to shining brightly in all our endeavors. Just as stars illuminate the night sky, our guild aims to stand out through our inclusiveness, growth, and fairness. The name “Stellar” serves as a constant reminder of our aspiration to be a guiding light in the World of Warcraft community, fostering a positive and supportive environment for all members.

Stellar leadership

What we stand for

Our values

Inclusiveness

Every skill level, every background. If you're here to play and be decent to people, you belong here.

Growth

We help each other get better, with honest feedback and patient teaching, whether that's your first raid or your hundredth key.

Fairness

Clear rules, open loot decisions, and the same standards for officers and new members alike.

The motto

Train. Compete. Evolve.

Train

Learn your class, study the fights, and ask questions. Officers and veterans are here to help, not to gatekeep.

Compete

Push progression, climb ranked, and chase the hard stuff together, as a team that holds a high bar.

Evolve

Every season, every patch, every game, we want to be a little better than we were.

Community guidelines

What we expect

  • Treat every member with respect. No harassment, slurs, or personal attacks.
  • Feedback is about the gameplay, never the person. Give it kindly, take it well.
  • Show up prepared for scheduled content, and let an officer know if you can't make it.
  • Loot and spots are handled openly. If something feels unfair, bring it to an officer.
  • Keep each game's chat in its own channel.

Find your place among the stars

New players, returning players, and veterans are all welcome. Say hi in the Discord and we'll get you set up.
